MEMORY TEST ENCLOSURE
Elecrow 5-inch LCD Display-B Rev 1.1 and Raspberry Pi 3 Model A+

SCHOOL PRINT HANDOFF

Import the STL files at 100% scale in millimeters. Use the school printer's
profile to generate its print file. The models are on the build plane.

01-body.stl                       Main shell, roof down on the bed
02-bezel.stl                      Front face, flat
03-lid-with-pi-mount.stl          Bottom lid, standoffs up
04-diffuser.stl                   Light diffuser, flat
05-display-connector-fit-test.stl Optional partial body for a quick fit check

The body needs about 169 x 111 x 101 mm of build space before adding a brim.
Use the school's filament profile. PLA and 0.20 mm layers are a reasonable
starting point for a filament printer. Use translucent filament for the
diffuser if it will cover a light. Preview supports at the body openings,
screen mounting pads and roof details. Choose supports you can remove
through the screen opening or open base.

BEFORE PRINTING THE FULL BODY

Print the optional fit section. It reproduces the HDMI-side upper mount
and cable relief. Test it with the real screen, the low-profile Type-A
HDMI plug and the micro-USB data plug. Turn the screen on and check the
bezel against the visible pixels. Connector bodies vary, so a successful
geometry check does not establish that a particular cable fits.

ASSEMBLY

1. Fit the screen from the front with HDMI and USB on the right when
   viewed from the front. The four ear pads are 8.7 mm behind the
   front face. Use thin nylon washers under the ears as needed to
   bring the glass level with the front face, without clamping it.
   Use M2.5 thread-forming screws for plastic, sized to the actual
   washer stack and ear thickness. The pilot holes are about 5 mm deep.
2. Fit the Pi on the four lid standoffs with its power and HDMI sockets
   facing the right wall. Use M2.5 thread-forming screws for plastic.
   The blind pilot holes are about 6 mm deep. Insert the microSD card
   while the lid is off.
3. Use full-size HDMI Type-A at both ends. A slim right-angle plug at
   the screen should turn away from the glass into the box. Connect
   either screen Touch port to the Pi with a USB-A to micro-USB data
   cable. Bring the Pi's power cable through the side opening.
4. The bezel and lid use M3-size fasteners. Choose screws suitable for
   printed plastic and check their length against the blind pilots
   before tightening. Fit the diffuser into the top recess if used.

The exported STLs were checked as closed meshes. The case parts and
nominal electronics envelopes were checked digitally. The real screen
stack height, image alignment and molded plug fit need a physical check.
